# 📊 Customer Churn & Business Intelligence Analytics Suite (Power BI)

A comprehensive multi-page Power BI reporting solution designed to analyze customer behavior, subscription trends, billing preferences, and churn risk factors to help businesses optimize retention strategies.

---

## 🌟 Overview

This project provides an end-to-end exploratory data analysis of customer retention and revenue metrics. Built using Power BI, the interactive dashboard is structured into **four core analytical pages**:
1. **Customer Churn Analysis** – High-level executive overview tracking overall churn rates, revenue impact, and key churn drivers.
2. **Customer Demographics** – Deep dive into customer distribution by gender, senior citizen status, tenure segments, and monthly charges[cite: 3].
3. **Service Subscription Analysis** – Evaluation of customer adoption across internet services, phone services, and total service counts[cite: 2].
4. **Contract & Billing Analysis** – Granular breakdown of payment methods, paperless billing preferences, and contract types correlated with churn.

---

## 📸 Dashboard Preview & Page Breakdown

### 1. Customer Churn Analysis
* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Tracks Total Customers (~7K), Churners (2K / 26.54%), overall Churn Rate (0.27), Average Monthly Charges, and Average Tenure (32.37 months)[cite: 4].
* **Visual Insights:** Displays churn proportions, contract vulnerability (Month-to-month contracts exhibit the highest churn at 0.43), and churn distribution across tenure brackets[cite: 4].

### 2. Customer Demographics
* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Analyzes customer profiles segmented by gender, senior citizen status, partner/dependents status, and tenure lengths[cite: 3].
* **Visual Insights:** Identifies concentration clusters, tracks average monthly spend against tenure cohorts, and highlights demographic risk factors[cite: 3].

### 3. Service Subscription Analysis
* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Examines product adoption metrics, separating users by internet service types (e.g., Fiber optic) and phone service dependencies[cite: 2].
* **Visual Insights:** Visualizes churn rates relative to the number of active services utilized by a customer and maps out service-specific revenue performance[cite: 2].

### 4. Contract & Billing Analysis
* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Focuses on financial retention vectors including contract length, billing methods, and payment types[cite: 1].
* **Visual Insights:** Compares average total charges across payment methods (e.g., Electronic check vs. Bank transfer) and highlights the impact of paperless billing on customer attrition[cite: 1].

---

## 🛠️ Key Features & Interactivity

* **Dynamic Navigation Pane:** Seamless sidebar buttons allowing users to effortlessly switch between Churn, Demographics, Subscription, and Billing perspectives.
* **Global Slicers & Filters:** Interactive filters enabled for **Gender**, **Contract Type**, **Internet Service**, **Tenure Segment**, **Payment Method**, and **Paperless Billing** for deep exploratory filtering.
* **Custom Dark Theme UI:** Designed with a modern, high-contrast dark visual aesthetic, neon accents, and neatly organized metric cards.
